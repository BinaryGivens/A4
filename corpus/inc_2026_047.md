# INC-2026-047

**Opened:** 2026-03-14 19:22 UTC
**Closed:** 2026-03-26 14:00 UTC
**Severity:** High
**Reporting analyst:** Sofia Brandt (Tier 2)

## Summary

The Foundry CI audit log on VAN-SRV-BLD02 recorded an administrative login and a pipeline
configuration change at 19:06 UTC. The session authenticated as the domain account
`dwhitfield` and originated from VAN-LAP-0912 over the contractor remote access path. The
pipeline change added a post-build step fetching a script from an external address.

## Affected hosts

- **VAN-SRV-BLD02** - Foundry CI build server, target of the administrative session.
- **VAN-LAP-0912** - contractor laptop, source of the session.

## Techniques observed

- T1078.002 Valid Accounts: Domain Accounts.
- T1105 Ingress Tool Transfer - the post-build step fetched an external script.

## Analyst notes

The account `dwhitfield` should not have been able to authenticate on 2026-03-14. Its
engagement ended on 2026-03-06 and the offboarding change record was never executed, so the
account remained enabled and retained its group memberships.

Sofia Brandt noted that two separate controls would each have prevented this session: a
completed offboarding change, or the removal of the contractor exception permitting VPN
sessions to reach SEG-ENG. Neither was in place.

Vandelay has not established whether the person Dana Whitfield used the account or whether
the credentials were in the hands of a third party. The evidence in this ticket does not
distinguish the two.

## The two conditions that made this possible

The session required both a live account and a network path, and both were available.

The account was live because the offboarding change record raised on 2026-03-03 was never
executed. The engagement ended on 2026-03-06. The account was still enabled on 2026-03-14.

The network path existed because contractor remote access sessions are permitted to reach
the engineering segment under an exception granted in January 2026 with no expiry. Without
that exception the session would have landed in the corporate segment and stopped there.

## The failed attempt against the payroll database

At 19:22 UTC the same account attempted to authenticate to VAN-SRV-DB07 and failed with
status 0xC000006D. This attempt is the clearest evidence in the campaign that the actor was
looking for payroll data. It failed because the account is not a member of the group that
holds payroll database entitlements, and because the segmentation model does not permit the
corporate segment to reach the finance core segment. Either control alone would have been
sufficient.

## Remediation

The pipeline configuration was reverted on 2026-03-15 and the build server was rebuilt from
a known-good image on 2026-03-17. The external script referenced by the post-build step was
never fetched; the pipeline did not run between the change and the revert.

The account `dwhitfield` was disabled on 2026-03-15 under an emergency out-of-band action
rather than through CHG-2026-0884, which was still deferred at that point.
