# INC-2026-046

**Opened:** 2026-03-09 02:31 UTC
**Closed:** 2026-03-15 10:20 UTC
**Severity:** Medium
**Reporting analyst:** Kenji Watanabe (Tier 1)

## Summary

Database auditing on VAN-SRV-DB07 recorded 41,900 row reads against the payroll employee
table between 01:50 and 02:20 UTC, roughly twelve times the nightly baseline. The
connection authenticated as the service account svc_payroll_sync and originated from
VAN-SRV-JMP01.

## Affected hosts

- **VAN-SRV-DB07** - Meridian Payroll DB, target of the queries.
- **VAN-SRV-JMP01** - administrative jump host, source of the connection.

## Techniques observed

- T1078.002 Valid Accounts: Domain Accounts.

## Analyst notes

The nightly payroll reconciliation job normally runs from VAN-SRV-APP12, not from the
administrative jump host. Kenji Watanabe queried IT Operations and Alan Ferris confirmed
that the reconciliation job had been temporarily relocated to VAN-SRV-JMP01 on 2026-03-07
during Kestrel HRIS maintenance, and that the elevated row count was caused by a
full-table reconciliation rather than the usual incremental pass.

The incident was closed as a false positive on 2026-03-15. It is retained in the corpus
because it establishes that svc_payroll_sync can authenticate to the Meridian Payroll DB
from the administrative jump host, and because the elevated read volume was not
attributable to SILVER TAMARIN.

No evidence of data exfiltration from VAN-SRV-DB07 was found in this incident or in any
other incident during the campaign.

## Baseline comparison

The nightly incremental reconciliation reads approximately 3,400 rows. A full-table
reconciliation reads every row in the employee table, which stood at 41,900 records on
2026-03-09. The observed volume is therefore exactly one full table, not an excess.

The connection source 10.60.1.9 is the administrative jump host. The reconciliation job
normally runs from the HRIS application server. The change of source, not the row count, is
what made the alert worth a ticket.

## Why the job moved

The relocation was not a security decision. The HRIS application logged a fatal scheduler
error during the maintenance window on 2026-03-07 and the documented remediation for that
error is to move the job to an alternate host. Alan Ferris chose the administrative jump
host because it already had a database client installed and held the necessary network path.
The choice was never reviewed by the SOC and was not recorded as a change.

## Why this ticket is retained

Closed false positives are normally purged after thirty days. This one is retained for two
reasons. It documents that svc_payroll_sync can authenticate to the Meridian Payroll DB from
the administrative jump host, a path that is not obvious from the access review alone. And it
is the only ticket in the campaign window that touches the payroll database at all, which
matters when answering whether payroll data was ever at risk.

Kenji Watanabe noted that if the actor had ever reached the administrative jump host, this
same path would have been available to them. The actor never did.
