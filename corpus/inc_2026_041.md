# INC-2026-041

**Opened:** 2026-02-16 08:42 UTC
**Closed:** 2026-02-27 16:10 UTC
**Initial severity:** Medium
**Final severity:** Critical
**Reporting analyst:** Kenji Watanabe (Tier 1)
**Escalated to:** Sofia Brandt (Tier 2)

## Summary

A spearphishing email carrying a macro-enabled spreadsheet was delivered to three Finance
mailboxes on 2026-02-16. One recipient opened the attachment. The macro launched an
encoded PowerShell command that harvested browser-stored credentials and posted them to a
remote host. This is the first confirmed intrusion of the campaign.

## Affected hosts

- **VAN-WKS-4471** - attachment opened at 08:31 UTC, macro executed, credential harvesting
  confirmed. This is the only host on which the payload executed.
- VAN-WKS-2210 - email delivered, attachment not opened. No execution.

## Techniques observed

- T1566.001 Spearphishing Attachment - the initial macro-enabled spreadsheet.
- T1059.001 PowerShell - encoded command launched by the macro.
- T1552.001 Unsecured Credentials: Credentials In Files - browser credential store read.

## Analyst notes

Kenji Watanabe initially triaged this as Medium on the basis that no lateral movement was
seen and the mailbox owner reset their password the same morning. Sofia Brandt reviewed the
case on 2026-02-19 and kept the Medium rating pending further evidence.

The severity of INC-2026-041 was raised from Medium to Critical on 2026-03-05, once the
post-incident review established that the credentials harvested here were reused in
INC-2026-045. The Medium rating was correct on the evidence available in February and
incorrect in hindsight.

Only the workstation VAN-WKS-4471 executed the payload. Credentials for the interactive
user of that workstation were confirmed stolen.

## Containment and remediation

The message was removed from all three mailboxes and the sender domain was added to the
mail gateway blocklist. The interactive user of VAN-WKS-4471 changed their password through
the self-service portal at 09:20 UTC. No forced re-enrolment was performed and no session
tokens were revoked, which the post-incident review later identified as the gap that let the
credentials remain useful for seventeen days.

VAN-WKS-4471 was not re-imaged. The EDR platform reported no persistence artefacts on the
host and the macro process tree terminated cleanly, so IT Operations judged a re-image
unnecessary. That judgement was not revisited.

## Evidence retained

Mail gateway transaction logs, the attachment sample, the EDR process tree export for
2026-02-16, and the proxy record of the outbound POST. All items are held under the standard
ninety-day retention, extended to one year on 2026-03-20 under CHG-2026-0899.

## Open questions at closure

Kenji Watanabe recorded two open questions at closure. First, whether the same lookalike
domain had been used against Vandelay before. Second, what the harvested credentials would
grant if reused. The second question was not answered before the ticket was closed.
