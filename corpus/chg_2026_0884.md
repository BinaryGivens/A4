# CHG-2026-0884

**Title:** Contractor offboarding - disable `dwhitfield` and remove group memberships
**Raised by:** Alan Ferris, IAM Administrator
**Raised on:** 2026-03-03
**Planned execution:** 2026-03-09
**Status:** NOT COMPLETED

## Scope

- Disable domain account `dwhitfield`.
- Remove `dwhitfield` from GRP-ENG-BUILD.
- Remove `dwhitfield` from GRP-VPN-CONTRACTOR.
- Revoke remote access certificate.
- Mark VAN-LAP-0912 for collection.

## Execution log

- 2026-03-03: Change record raised and queued for the Monday maintenance slot.
- 2026-03-09: Maintenance slot cancelled. The change advisory board deferred all
  non-emergency identity changes while INC-2026-045 was under active investigation, to
  avoid disturbing evidence on hosts under forensic hold.
- 2026-03-16: Still deferred. Alan Ferris noted that the deferral was never revisited.
- 2026-03-23: Reopened following INC-2026-047.

CHG-2026-0884 was never executed. As a result the domain account `dwhitfield` remained
enabled and retained membership of GRP-ENG-BUILD and GRP-VPN-CONTRACTOR beyond the
engagement end date recorded in the HR engagement end notice.

## Approvals

| Role | Name | Decision | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Requester | Alan Ferris | raised | 2026-03-03 |
| Line approver | Rachel Ocampo | approved | 2026-03-04 |
| Change advisory board | standing membership | deferred | 2026-03-09 |
| Change advisory board | standing membership | deferred | 2026-03-16 |

The change was approved on its merits within a day. Nothing about the content of the change
was ever disputed. Both deferrals were procedural, arising from the forensic hold in place on
hosts involved in INC-2026-045.

## Consequence

Between the engagement end date recorded in the HR notice and 2026-03-15, when the account
was disabled out of band, the domain account `dwhitfield` was enabled and held its full set of group
memberships for nine days past the end of the engagement. During that window the account was
used to log on to the build server, which is recorded in INC-2026-047.

## Why the deferral was not caught

The change management system flags a change as overdue only when its planned execution date
passes without a disposition. A deferral sets a new planned date, which resets the overdue
clock. Two successive deferrals therefore produced no overdue flag and no notification to the
requester, the approver or HR Operations.

Alan Ferris recorded on 2026-03-16 that he had assumed the board would revisit the freeze
when the forensic hold lifted, and that no one owned that revisit.

## Closure

CHG-2026-0884 was reopened on 2026-03-23 and closed as superseded on 2026-03-26, the
disablement having already been performed out of band on 2026-03-15. The group memberships
were removed on 2026-03-24 and the remote access certificate was revoked on 2026-03-24.
Laptop VAN-LAP-0912 had still not been physically recovered at closure.
