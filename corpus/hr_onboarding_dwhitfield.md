## Supplier framework terms

The Nimbus Consulting Group framework agreement, clause 11.4, requires that Vandelay disable
contractor accounts within one business day of the engagement end date and recover issued
equipment within five business days. Clause 11.6 places the obligation to initiate the
disablement on Vandelay, not on the supplier. Nimbus Consulting Group is required only to
notify Vandelay of the end date, which it did.
