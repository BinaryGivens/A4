# INC-2026-045

**Opened:** 2026-03-05 21:18 UTC
**Closed:** 2026-03-19 12:00 UTC
**Severity:** Critical
**Reporting analyst:** Sofia Brandt (Tier 2)

## Summary

An interactive remote desktop session was established to VAN-SRV-FILE03 from VAN-WKS-4471
at 21:04 UTC using valid domain credentials. The session ran for 38 minutes, enumerated
three departmental shares and staged 1.4 GB of documents into
`C:\Windows\Temp\arch\stage01.7z`. The staged archive was not transferred during the
session.

## Affected hosts

- **VAN-SRV-FILE03** - destination of the remote desktop session.
- **VAN-WKS-4471** - source of the session.

## Techniques observed

- T1078.002 Valid Accounts: Domain Accounts - the session used legitimate credentials, not
  an exploit.
- T1021.001 Remote Services: Remote Desktop Protocol.

## Analyst notes

The credentials used were those of the interactive user of VAN-WKS-4471, harvested during
INC-2026-041. This is the link that caused INC-2026-041 to be re-rated Critical.

Sofia Brandt placed VAN-SRV-FILE03 and VAN-WKS-4471 under forensic hold on 2026-03-06. The
hold triggered the change advisory board freeze on non-emergency identity changes that
caused CHG-2026-0884 to be deferred.

No access to the Meridian Payroll DB was observed during this incident. Reaching
SEG-FIN-CORE from SEG-CORP is not permitted by the segmentation model.

## Why this was not blocked

The session used valid domain credentials and a permitted protocol between two segments that
are allowed to talk. RDP from SEG-FIN-CORE into SEG-CORP is within the segmentation model.
Nothing in the session was anomalous at the network layer; the detection came from a
behavioural rule on the source account, which had never previously established an RDP
session to a server.

The credentials were harvested seventeen days earlier. Because the response to INC-2026-041
was a self-service password change rather than a forced re-enrolment with token revocation,
the actor's foothold survived that change.

## The archive

`stage01.7z` is 1.4 GB, password protected, and was not transferred during the session. It
remained on disk from 2026-03-05 until the exfiltration recorded in INC-2026-048 on
2026-03-21. Sixteen days passed between staging and exfiltration. No process on
VAN-SRV-FILE03 touched the archive in the interval.

The three shares enumerated were the Legal, Procurement and Programme Management
departmental shares. None of them contain payroll data. Payroll records live only in the
Meridian Payroll DB.

## Errors of omission

The archive was left in place under the forensic hold rather than being quarantined. The
campaign post-incident review records that quarantining it, or simply alerting on any
outbound transfer of a file of that size from that host, would have interrupted the
campaign before any data left the network.
