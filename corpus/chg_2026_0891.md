# CHG-2026-0891

**Title:** Emergency upgrade of remote access concentrator
**Raised by:** Rachel Ocampo, Director of IT Operations
**Raised on:** 2026-03-10
**Executed:** 2026-03-12
**Status:** COMPLETED

## Justification

Vendor advisory APERTURE-SA-2026-014 describes CVE-2026-3117, a pre-authentication remote
code execution flaw in Aperture SecureEdge VPN. Threat intelligence circulated by the SOC
attributes active exploitation of CVE-2026-3117 to the actor tracked as SILVER TAMARIN.
VAN-SRV-VPN01 was running Aperture SecureEdge VPN 7.2.1 and was therefore in the affected
range.

## Execution

On 2026-03-12 VAN-SRV-VPN01 was upgraded from Aperture SecureEdge VPN 7.2.1 to Aperture
SecureEdge VPN 7.2.6. The appliance was rebooted at 02:40 and remote access was restored
at 03:05. Post-upgrade verification confirmed the build string reported by the appliance is
7.2.6.

No configuration changes were made. The contractor remote access exception described in
Finding AR-02 of the Q1 access review remained in place after the upgrade.

## Follow-up

Rachel Ocampo asked that the asset inventory be re-issued to reflect the new version. The
inventory was re-issued as Revision 2 on 2026-03-16.

The change was raised, approved and executed under the emergency path, which permits the
requester to self-approve where a critical vulnerability is under active exploitation. The
board endorsed the emergency use retrospectively on 2026-03-16.

## Pre-change state and post-change state

| Attribute | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Software version | Aperture SecureEdge VPN 7.2.1 | Aperture SecureEdge VPN 7.2.6 |
| Affected by CVE-2026-3117 | yes | no |
| Management interface exposure | internet-reachable on 8443/tcp | unchanged |
| Contractor segment exception | active | active |

The upgrade closed the vulnerability and changed nothing else. In particular the management
interface remained reachable from the internet, and the contractor exception permitting VPN
sessions into the engineering segment remained active. That exception is what made the
session in INC-2026-047 possible two days later, and this change did nothing to address it.

## Backout plan

The prior firmware image was retained on the appliance's secondary partition. Backout would
have been a reboot to the secondary partition, approximately eight minutes of downtime. The
backout was not required.

## Verification

Post-upgrade the appliance build string was read from the console and from the management API
and both reported 7.2.6. The IDS signature 2101922 continued to fire against the appliance
after the upgrade, as expected, since the signature matches the request pattern and not a
successful outcome. Continued firings after 2026-03-12 do not indicate exposure.

## Timing

Twenty-six days elapsed between the publication of APERTURE-SA-2026-014 on 2026-02-14 and the
raising of this change on 2026-03-10.
