# INC-2026-042

**Opened:** 2026-02-19 03:14 UTC
**Closed:** 2026-02-21 11:00 UTC
**Severity:** High
**Reporting analyst:** Sofia Brandt (Tier 2)

## Summary

The perimeter IDS recorded 1,184 requests over four hours against the management interface
of VAN-SRV-VPN01 matching the exploitation pattern for CVE-2026-3117. Requests originated
from 203.0.113.19. No successful exploitation was confirmed; the appliance did not log a
crash or an unexpected process launch, and no session was established from the source
address.

## Affected hosts

- **VAN-SRV-VPN01** - target of the attempts. Running Aperture SecureEdge VPN 7.2.1 at the
  time, which is within the range affected by CVE-2026-3117.

## Techniques observed

- T1190 Exploit Public-Facing Application.

## Analyst notes

Sofia Brandt correlated 203.0.113.19 against the threat intelligence holdings and found it
listed as SILVER TAMARIN scanning infrastructure. The activity was assessed as
opportunistic scanning rather than targeted exploitation, because the same source address
was seen probing unrelated organisations in the same window.

The appliance was left at Aperture SecureEdge VPN 7.2.1 after this incident. No emergency
change was raised in February. Rachel Ocampo recorded that the upgrade would be handled in
the normal Q1 maintenance window.

The decision not to patch VAN-SRV-VPN01 in February is examined in the campaign
post-incident review.

## Evidence reviewed

Appliance system log for the full window, appliance session table before and after,
perimeter firewall connection records, and the IDS packet captures for a sample of forty
requests. The captures show a consistently malformed session negotiation payload matching
the pattern published with APERTURE-SA-2026-014.

The appliance uptime counter was continuous across the window, which rules out a crash and
restart. No new administrative session appears in the session table. No file system changes
were recorded on the appliance.

## Risk accepted

Sofia Brandt recommended an out-of-band upgrade. Rachel Ocampo accepted the risk of waiting
for the Q1 maintenance window on the grounds that the attempts had failed, that the appliance
was under active monitoring, and that an unplanned outage of remote access would affect every
remote worker. The risk acceptance was recorded against this ticket and carried no expiry
date or review trigger.

Restricting the management interface on 8443/tcp to a management network was discussed as a
mitigation and not implemented, because the appliance vendor's own support process requires
management access from the internet for remote diagnostics.

## Aftermath

Probing from 203.0.113.19 continued at a low rate through late February and into March. The
same source address appears again in the perimeter IDS export for 2026-03-01. The appliance
was finally upgraded on 2026-03-12, twenty-six days after the vendor advisory was published.
