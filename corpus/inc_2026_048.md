# INC-2026-048

**Opened:** 2026-03-21 04:40 UTC
**Closed:** 2026-03-28 09:00 UTC
**Severity:** Critical
**Reporting analyst:** Sofia Brandt (Tier 2)

## Summary

A 1.4 GB upload from VAN-SRV-FILE03 to a third-party cloud storage provider completed at
04:12 UTC. The uploaded object matches the size and creation time of the archive
`C:\Windows\Temp\arch\stage01.7z` staged during INC-2026-045. This is the only confirmed
data exfiltration of the campaign.

## Affected hosts

- **VAN-SRV-FILE03** - source of the upload.

## Techniques observed

- T1567.002 Exfiltration Over Web Service: Exfiltration to Cloud Storage.

## Analyst notes

The upload did not traverse 198.51.100.77, which had been blocked at the perimeter on
2026-03-11. The actor used a commodity cloud storage service reachable over 443/tcp, which
is permitted from SEG-CORP by policy.

The archive staged in INC-2026-045 contained departmental documents from three shares on
VAN-SRV-FILE03. It did not contain payroll records; the Meridian Payroll DB sits in
SEG-FIN-CORE and was not reachable from VAN-SRV-FILE03.

Total confirmed exfiltration across the campaign is 1.4 GB, from a single host, in a single
transfer.

## Attribution of the transfer

The uploading process ran as the same domain account used in INC-2026-045. The user agent
recorded by the proxy is `rclone/1.66`, a file transfer utility that is not part of the
Vandelay standard software build and was not present on the host before the campaign. The
archive size, its creation time and the total bytes uploaded all match, which is the basis
for tying this transfer to the staging recorded in INC-2026-045.

## Why the detection was late

The alert fired on cumulative outbound volume over a rolling one-hour window with a
threshold of 1 GB. The threshold was crossed at 04:07 UTC and the alert was raised at
04:40 UTC after the aggregation interval closed. By then the transfer was complete. A
per-request threshold, or an alert on any use of a non-standard transfer utility, would
have fired at 03:52 UTC.

## Scope of loss

The 1.4 GB comprises documents from the Legal, Procurement and Programme Management
departmental shares. Vandelay's data classification exercise rates the contents as
Confidential but not Restricted. No payroll records, no customer payment data and no source
code were included. Source code lives on the build server, which was accessed in
INC-2026-047 but from which no bulk transfer was recorded.

Legal and Compliance were notified on 2026-03-21 and determined on 2026-03-24 that the
contents did not trigger a regulatory notification obligation.
